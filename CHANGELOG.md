# Changelog

## v0.1.0-beta1

- First beta for World of Warcraft: Forever. Friendly nameplates show missing health (`-0` at full health) in place of Blizzard's health number. Everything else stays Blizzard's.
- Commands: `/hp-help`, `/hp-status`, `/hp-on`, `/hp-off`.
- Not yet tested inside dungeons and raids. Please report what you see there with `/hp-status`.
