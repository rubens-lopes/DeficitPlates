# Changelog

## v0.2.0-beta1

- Deficit Plates is now part of [Dynamic Display Nameplate](https://github.com/rubens-lopes/DynamicDisplayNameplate) (v0.3.0-beta1 and later): turn on "Missing health on friendly plates" there (`/ddn-deficit on`), then disable or uninstall Deficit Plates. This is the last release.
- Logging in prints a reminder of the above. The addon keeps working until you switch.

## v0.1.0-beta1

- First beta for World of Warcraft: Forever. Friendly nameplates show missing health (`-0` at full health) in place of Blizzard's health number. Everything else stays Blizzard's.
- Commands: `/dp-help`, `/dp-status`, `/dp-on`, `/dp-off`.
- Not yet tested inside dungeons and raids. Please report what you see there with `/dp-status`.
